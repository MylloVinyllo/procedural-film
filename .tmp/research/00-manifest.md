# Research capture manifest

Subject: the human life cycle.

Story phases:
1. biological beginning → NICHD;
2. infancy / childhood development → WHO life course;
3. adolescence / social salience → NIMH + WHO;
4. adulthood as individual plus social participant → WHO social connection + WHO life course;
5. ageing → WHO life course + WHO social connection;
6. intergenerational continuation → WHO life course.

The external web interface exposes extracted source text rather than a raw-page archive. These files preserve the source identity, open question, facts actually used, and the film boundary so downstream claims remain auditable.
