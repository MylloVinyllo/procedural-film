# WHO Social Connection capture
URL: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/978240112360
Checked: 2026-09-26
Authority: WHO Commission on Social Connection.

Open question: Is the social dimension substantive enough to be a core life-course axis rather than decorative crowd imagery?

Captured facts used by the film:
- social isolation and loneliness are widespread;
- they have consequential effects on health and well-being;
- strengthening social connection is treated as a meaningful public-health goal.

Film boundary: network size is not scored as “better”; close bonds and social participation are shown qualitatively.
