Authority: Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health and Human Development, NIH.

Open question: What factual sequence is safe to depict at biological beginning?

Captured facts used by the film:
- pregnancy events begin with conception;
- a fertilized egg is called a zygote;
- the zygote travels toward the uterus and is a cluster of cells;
- those cells later contribute to fetus and placenta.

Film boundary: the animation does not assign exact hours/days to its 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 symbolic division sequence.
