# Storyboard · Human Life Cycle

## Logline

One human life emerges from a single biological beginning, develops an individual trajectory, becomes entangled with other lives, ages, and passes continuity forward.

Method: warm hand-inked lived moments alternate with cool blueprint views of cells, learning, social systems and memory; recurring circles, trajectories and match cuts make the cycle structural rather than decorative.

## Numbers

- BPM: 120
- Beat: 0.5 s = 12 frames
- Bar: 2.0 s = 48 frames
- Duration: 32.0 s = 16 bars = 768 frames
- Shot count: 16
- Every shot: 2.0 s, exactly one bar

## Structure

Act 1 · bars 1–4 · 0–8 s
Biological beginning becomes an embodied human.

Act 2 · bars 5–8 · 8–16 s
Individual development: movement, learning, exploration, identity.

Act 3 · bars 9–12 · 16–24 s
Individual and social human occupy the same life: crowd, network, bond, contribution.

Act 4 · bars 13–16 · 24–32 s
Memory, ageing, intergenerational handoff and return.

Midpoint hinge:
T 16.0, exactly bar 9 downbeat, when the solitary developmental arc enters the social field.

Ending:
T 32 composition matches the opening seed-pulse geometry closely enough to loop.
