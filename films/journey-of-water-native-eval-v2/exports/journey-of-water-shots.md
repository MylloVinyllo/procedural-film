# The Journey of Water — shot list

01. **Held in the cloud** — a precipitation-ready drop gathers inside a layered cloud field.
02. **Droplets gather** — cloud microdroplets condense around nuclei and coalesce around the tracked parcel.
03. **Heavy enough to fall** — airflow and deformation explain why a larger falling raindrop flattens.
04. **Through the air** — the tracked drop falls through layered rain toward vegetation.
05. **Impact** — the drop strikes a leaf, splashes, spreads and becomes a surface lens.
06. **Another path** — a leaf cross-section shows transpiration as an alternate route while the tracked parcel stays on the surface path.
07. **Into the ground** — water reaches the forest floor and infiltrates through roots, aggregates and stones.
08. **Through pores** — a pore-network schematic shows unsaturated and saturated subsurface flow.
09. **Surface again** — groundwater emerges as a spring and joins a moving creek.
10. **Becoming a river** — the camera pulls from creek scale to a larger tributary river system.
11. **A larger system** — a watershed view shows river flow and partial diversion into a public-water intake.
12. **Made usable** — drinking-water treatment proceeds through mixing/flocculation, settling, filtration, contact and storage.
13. **Under the city** — treated water moves through a pressurised main and a smaller service branch.
14. **Through a home** — water leaves a faucet, interacts with a glass and sink, then turns into drain flow.
15. **Cleaned again** — wastewater passes screening, settling, biological treatment, clarification and a clear effluent stage.
16. **Back to the river** — treated effluent mixes into receiving water and becomes one local streamline.
17. **Into the ocean** — river flow opens into an estuary and the ocean, then the camera approaches a sunlit surface patch.
18. **Leaving the surface** — evaporation is shown as liquid structure losing coherence while vapor points separate and rise.
19. **Becoming cloud** — vapor reaches nuclei, condenses into microdroplets and thickens into a cloud field.
20. **The cycle continues** — the cloud returns to the opening visual language and forms a new precipitation-ready drop for the loop.
