## Relevant facts captured

- Collection sewers and pumping systems carry wastewater from homes, businesses and industry to treatment plants.
- Typical municipal treatment uses preliminary screening/grit removal, primary settling, secondary biological treatment, and disinfection; exact trains vary by plant.
- Treated effluent is commonly discharged to receiving waters or reused.
- In primary treatment, flow is slowed so settleable solids sink.
- Secondary treatment uses biological processes to remove biodegradable organic material and suspended solids.

## Drawing implications

If the story follows water after domestic use, the return leg should show a sewer/collection pipe followed by a simplified but truthful treatment sequence before discharge to a river or other receiving water. It should not jump directly from a drain to a pristine river.
