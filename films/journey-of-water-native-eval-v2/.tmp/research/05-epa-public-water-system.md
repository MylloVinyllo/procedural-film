## Relevant facts captured

- Public drinking-water systems draw from surface water such as lakes, rivers and reservoirs or from groundwater.
- Source water is treated before distribution.
- Common conventional treatment stages include coagulation/flocculation, sedimentation, filtration and disinfection.
- After treatment, water may be stored and then pumped through water mains and smaller service lines to homes and businesses.

## Drawing implications

The human-infrastructure sequence should show a treatment train rather than a generic glowing pipe. The tracked water can pass through flocculation/sedimentation, a layered filter, storage, a large main and a smaller service pipe.
