## Required product-specific animation cues

When the machine first appears:
- front plate and all four button positions should be readable;
- at least one illuminated ring should respond to the active stage;
- record rotation reflection should make motion obvious;
- working node must physically pivot/settle onto the record before its corresponding process effect begins.

For PUMP stage:
- supply/brush node on left is engaged;
- PUMP button receives active highlight;
- liquid appears at/under the brush/feed contact, not from nowhere.

For REVERSE:
- REVERSE button can flash briefly;
- record highlight / label direction visibly changes.

For VACUUM:
- supply node is parked away;
- collection node on right engages;
- VACUUM button lights;
- wet film visibly converges toward the contact slot;
- dry record emerges behind the contact path.

## Visual-quality implication

The machine should not be drawn as a single black rectangle with two rods.
Recognition depends on the combined silhouette:
black box + record/clamp + two asymmetric node types + branded four-button front plate.
