# V3 research phase map

Date: 2026-09-27

The native Research step requires every phase of the subject's story to trace to captured sources.

## Phase 1 — discovery and handling

Story:
The protagonist removes / holds an old record and inspects it.

Source support:
- `01-loc-handling.md`

Supported visual facts:
- hold grooved records by edge and label areas;
- do not touch the playing surface.

Narrative invention:
- the record is discovered in a shelf/sleeve at home.

## Phase 2 — visible contamination

Story:
The protagonist sees dust / dirt on the playable surface.

Source support:
- `03-ortofon-care.md`
- `02-loc-cleaning.md`

Supported visual facts:
- dust/dirt on records is a legitimate cleaning concern;
- preservation workflows remove dust/microscopic particles from grooves.

Narrative constraint:
- do not equate every visible speck with one exact audible defect.

## Phase 3 — first playback

Story:
Record on platter, platter rotates, tonearm/stylus descends, listener hears unpleasant surface noise.

Source support:
- `04-project-technics-operation.md`
- `03-ortofon-care.md`

Supported physical action:
- record placement / rotation / tonearm movement / stylus contact.

Supported care context:
- dust/dirt affects record/stylus care and listening experience.

Narrative invention:
- this particular record has an audible crackle/noise problem before cleaning.

## Phase 4 — decision to clean

Story:
The listener stops playback, visually links the problem to contamination, and prepares a cleaning setup.

Source support:
- `03-ortofon-care.md`
- `01-loc-handling.md`

Supported action:
- periodic record cleaning / brushing / machine cleaning is legitimate.

Narrative invention:
- the protagonist decides to solve the problem immediately.

## Phase 5 — wet / brush / vacuum cleaning

Story:
Fluid is distributed; a soft brush works the rotating surface; vacuum removes liquid; disc is left dry.

Source support:
- `02-loc-cleaning.md`
- `04-project-technics-operation.md`

Supported sequence:
- cleaning solution;
- soft brush / controlled contact;
- rotation;
- vacuum removal;
- drying;
- repeat by side when needed.

V3 simplification:
- show one side of one record clearly rather than every maintenance detail.

## Phase 6 — second playback

Story:
The same playback geometry is repeated after cleaning.

Source support:
- `04-project-technics-operation.md`
- `03-ortofon-care.md`

Supported physical action:
- same record / platter / tonearm / stylus sequence.

Narrative invention:
- this record's audible surface noise is reduced enough after cleaning to create a satisfying before/after contrast.

Constraint:
- do not state or imply that cleaning repairs scratches, groove wear or every source of noise.

## Phase 7 — listening reward

Story:
The protagonist relaxes and enjoys the restored listening experience.

Source support:
- no technical claim required.

This is the emotional resolution of the fictional micro-story.

## Research closure

All story phases now trace either:
- to one or more captured authoritative sources for physical/maintenance facts, or
- explicitly to a narrative invention that is not presented as a general technical claim.

Research can now flow into:
1. `docs/art-bible.md` subject reference;
2. the Subject subsection of each storyboard shot.
