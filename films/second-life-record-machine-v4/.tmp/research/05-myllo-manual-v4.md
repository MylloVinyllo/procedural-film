## Machine identity / technical passport

Manual page 30 identifies:
- product: vacuum cleaner for vinyl records;
- model: **Myllo Vinyllo**;
- manufacturer: ФОП Ракочій В.І. (Ukraine);
- dimensions: **350 × 350 × 200 mm**;
- maximum record size: 12 inches;
- nominal wash cycle: approximately **3–5 minutes**;
- water/cleaning reservoir: **500 ml**;
- total power: 500 W.

Manual page 31 identifies:
- vacuum pump power: **400 W**;
- brushes: **2**;
- body material: **metal**.

These numbers are not intended for on-screen explanatory text in V4. They are useful to constrain proportions and product silhouette.

## Named hardware / controls

The manual names:

The operating pages name four front-panel actions:
- **START**
- **PUMP**
- **REVERSE** (manual typography sometimes prints REVERCE)
- **VACUUM**

## Source-derived cleaning sequence

### Rotation / start

Manual page 13:
- press **START** on the front panel;
- the record begins rotating.

### Bring the supply node into working position

Manual page 13:
- move the supply node onto the record surface;
- the node must be lifted slightly before turning it into position.

Manual page 14:
- once returned to working position, the supply node's internal spring presses its brush against the record surface.

### Apply cleaning liquid

Manual page 14:
- press and hold **PUMP** until liquid is distributed over the record surface and the brush is fully wet;
- usually only several seconds are required once the feed path is primed.

### Brush cleaning / direction change

Manual page 14:
- a standard cleaning cycle uses roughly 60 seconds of rotation/brush cleaning in each direction.

Manual pages 15/17:
- **REVERSE** changes the direction of record rotation.

For a 30–36 s film, V4 may compress the real duration but must preserve the causal order:
rotation → liquid/brush contact → reverse direction.

### Move the supply node away before vacuum collection

Manual page 15:
- after brush cleaning, move the supply node back/out of the working position.

### Position collection node

Manual page 15:
- move the collection node onto the record so it points toward the centre;
- do **not** switch on vacuum before the collection node is correctly positioned on the record.

### Vacuum collection

Manual page 15:
- press **VACUUM**;
- the collection node presses to the vinyl record;
- liquid from the record surface and its grooves, together with contamination, is sucked away by vacuum.

Manual page 16:
- vacuum collection can be performed with a direction change;
- pressing VACUUM again ends the collection cycle;
- wait for vacuum force to fall and for the spring/node to release before moving the collection node away.

### Other side

Manual page 17:
- stop rotation with START;
- release/remove clamp;
- turn the record over;
- repeat the full wash cycle on the other side.

V4 does not need to show the second side unless the storyboard needs the complete-cycle truth. It must not imply that one brief brush swipe equals the full real-world cycle.

## Product-description language from the manual

Manual page 30 states that:
- the built-in vacuum removes dirt and water from the record surface and grooves;
- the machine has automatic liquid feed to the record;
- the supply system uses a double brush on a floating base;
- the record can rotate in different directions;
- control is by buttons on the front panel.

## V4 source boundaries

Supported:
- visible rotation;
- supply node / brush;
- pump button / liquid feed;
- reversal;
- collection node;
- vacuum removal of liquid + contamination;
- dark metal body is supported by product video, not the manual text alone.

Do not invent:
- automatic timing not shown in the manual;
- glowing contamination extraction;
- ultrasonic action;
- a second vacuum arm;
- a fully automatic cycle for this legacy Myllo Vinyllo model.
