# Library of Congress — grooved-disc handling

Checked: 2026-09-27

Source:
https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-recording-preservation-plan/tools-and-resources/care-handling-and-storage-of-audio-visual-materials/

## Source-derived facts

- Grooved discs such as LPs should be handled by the outer edge and label areas rather than the playing surface.
- Playback equipment should be kept clean and maintained.
- Records should be kept in a clean environment and away from food/drink.
- Disc storage is normally vertical rather than stacked.

## Story implications

The protagonist should visibly avoid touching the groove field.

Good hand poses:
- thumb at label + fingers at outer edge;
- two hands at opposite outer edges;
- sleeve handling that lets the disc slide out without palm contact on grooves.

Bad hand pose:
- flat fingertips across the playable black surface.

The opening discovery scene can show the protagonist noticing dirt while still handling the record correctly.

## Do not infer

This source does not say that every visible speck automatically causes audible crackle. V3 may show visible contamination as a reason to inspect/clean, but the exact audible defect must be staged conservatively.
