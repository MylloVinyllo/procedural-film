# Storyboard: Human Life Cycle

## Logline

One individual life begins, develops, enters a web of other lives, ages, and passes continuity forward while remaining visually distinct inside the social field.
The method alternates hand-inked paper life moments with blueprint views of cells, development, social systems and memory, using recurring geometry and match cuts so cyclicity is structural rather than decorative.

## Numbers

- 120 bpm
- beat = 0.5 s = 12 frames
- bar = 2.0 s = 48 frames
- duration = 32.0 s = 16 bars = 768 frames at 24 fps
- 18 shots
- every shot is 1.0 to 2.5 s and every boundary lies on the 0.5 s beat grid

## Structure

- Act 1, bars 1–4, T 0–8: the finished human hooks the film, then the story dives back to biological beginning and rapid developmental scale.
- Act 2, bars 5–8, T 8–16: individual trajectory, learning, childhood exploration and adolescent social salience.
- Act 3, bars 9–12, T 16–24: midpoint hinge into “We”, close bonds, social roles and contribution.
- Act 4, bars 13–16, T 24–32: accumulated traces, ageing, intergenerational continuity, handoff and loop.

Midpoint downbeat: T 16.000, shot 10 starts on G3.

Plate pattern: illustrated / schematic alternates for all 18 shots.

Match cuts:
- 01 → 02: G1 cycle halo becomes founding-cell guide.
- 04 → 05 → 06: G2 head/proportion anchor bridges growth comparison, first steps and learning network.
- 08 → 09 → 10: G3 primary social node becomes the hero’s chest marker, then the midpoint network node.
- 16 → 17 → 18: G4 transfer point passes between generations, then expands to G1.
- 18 → 01: G1 closes the loop into the opening adult halo.

Time devices:
- 18-arc progress glyph in every schematic shot, canonical helper owned by shot 02.
- selfWarm trajectory persists across illustrated life stages.
- memoryViolet traces reappear in shot 14.
- final loop returns to the opening G1 composition.

Act stripe B:
- Act 1: stripeApricot
- Act 2: stripeSky
- Act 3: stripeSage
- Act 4: stripeSpring / stripeApricot in later-life shot 15

Safe area review:
All must-read subjects, match-cut geometry, progress glyph and wordmark remain inside x 60–940, y 220–1540. Decorative scenery alone may exceed it.

## Conventions

- T is global seconds, t is shot-local seconds.
- Camera moves use lib.camera; shared geometry is screen-fixed.
- Hard cuts are default.
- Character/object motion is on twos; camera and overlays remain 24 fps.
- Schematic scenes contain no text except the final wordmark.

## Shots

Production rule for this revision: a shot is not considered implemented merely because its semantic idea is visible. Every illustrated shot must stage a physical event inside an inhabited environment; every schematic shot must contain internal structure and layered guide geometry. The scene implementation must later prove the shot with a six-sample contact sheet.

## 01 adult-hero: One life

T 0.000 to 1.500, illustrated, hard cut.

### Composition

Cold open on a fully drawn adult protagonist, not an icon. The figure stands three-quarter to camera, slightly left foot forward, centred around x=540 with head near y=610 and feet around y=1360. G1 sits behind upper torso/head, but the halo is partly occluded by the body so it belongs to the scene rather than floating above it.

Foreground: two plant/grass stems and a low curb/path edge cut into the lower 10% of frame.  
Midground: protagonist in rust-orange upper garment, dark lower garment, readable hands and shoes.  
Background: three social figures in two depth bands, a bench edge and distant façade/window rectangles. They are present but subordinate.

### Forms

Adult uses the six-mass construction in Art Bible §10.7. Warm garment has collar, hem, folds and hatching. Hair silhouette is asymmetrical and becomes the persistent identity cue. Background figures use varied poses and garment tints. G1 is cycleGold with a faint outer construction ring.

### Overlays

A selfWarm trajectory enters from lower-left behind the feet, makes one loose turn and terminates at the protagonist. A thin annYellow ring expands from G1 only on the 0.500 beat.

### Motion

- T 0.000: complete pose and environment already readable.
- T 0.250: jacket hem and rear hand settle on twos.
- T 0.500: weight shifts to front foot; attention ring pops.
- T 0.750: far-left figure passes behind the protagonist.
- T 1.000: two background figures briefly match stride while the protagonist remains half a phase off.
- T 1.250: protagonist turns head slightly toward screen-right.
- T 1.375: camera begins a decisive push toward G1; foreground path drops out of frame.

### Camera

Mostly locked at 1.00. At T 1.375 push rapidly to about 1.35 while compensating so G1 centre remains exactly screen-fixed at (540,700).

### Enter and exit

Film opens fully drawn. Exit strips the environment away through scale while G1 remains exact, becoming shot 02's outer cell guide.

### Subject

WHO life-course framing supports adult life as one stage within a longer trajectory and explicitly includes social/environmental context. Solitary visual emphasis is only the opening hook.

### Sound

- T 0.000: felt-heart kick + soft paper brush.
- T 0.500: warm pluck and cloth/foot shift.
- T 1.000: two small spatial social ticks.
- T 1.375: reverse swell into cell-blueprint cut.

## 02 cell-genesis: Beginning

T 1.500 to 4.000, schematic, hard cut.

### Composition

G1 is the outer membrane/guide centred at (540,700). The cell is not an isolated glowing UI circle: it sits inside a faint tissue/fluid field made of two broad contour bands, 30–40 small drifting particulate marks and a rectangular/hexagonal construction lattice that fades toward frame edges. The canonical 18-arc progress glyph sits at (900,300).

### Forms

### Overlays

### Motion

### Camera

Locked. Apparent transformation comes from geometry, not zoom.

### Enter and exit

Incoming G1 is pixel-identical to shot 01. The final vertical axis lands on the newborn torso direction in shot 03.

### Subject

NICHD supports the zygote/cluster-of-cells basis. The 1→2→4→8 timing is explicitly a symbolic visual sequence, not a literal developmental clock.

### Sound
