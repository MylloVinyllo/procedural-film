# Human Life Cycle · shot guide

01. **adult-hero** — One adult life appears first, visually distinct but already surrounded by distant social presence.
02. **cell-genesis** — The film dives back to a founding cell and a symbolic 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 division sequence.
03. **birth** — A newborn arrives into the lived world through breath, gesture and an opening enclosure.
04. **growth-ladder** — Blueprint silhouettes compress infancy, childhood, adolescence and adulthood into one growth comparison.
05. **first-steps** — A child takes uncertain steps while the individual selfWarm trajectory begins.
06. **learning-network** — The child’s head becomes a symbolic learning network with growing and pruning connections.
07. **childhood-explore** — The child runs and reaches upward, making individual exploration physically visible.
08. **social-salience** — Peer nodes enter a schematic field and attention turns outward toward them.
09. **one-among-many** — The primary person joins a moving group, briefly diverges from its rhythm, then remains distinct inside it.
10. **social-network** — At the midpoint, the same person becomes the warm core inside a heterogeneous network of relationships.
11. **bond** — Sociality returns to human scale as a reciprocal gesture between two people.
12. **role-system** — Overlapping schematic rings show one individual participating in several social contexts.
13. **create-contribute** — Individual making becomes shared contribution when another person receives and extends the action.
14. **memory-field** — Earlier paths, relationships and contribution reappear as accumulated traces around the same life.
15. **ageing-connected** — The same person ages through slower posture and tempo while remaining socially connected.
16. **generation-spiral** — Older and younger life arcs converge on an intergenerational transfer point.
17. **handoff** — Continuity passes forward, the older path resolves, and a new individual path begins.
18. **seed-loop** — The transfer point becomes the opening cycle geometry again, closing the film into a replayable loop.
