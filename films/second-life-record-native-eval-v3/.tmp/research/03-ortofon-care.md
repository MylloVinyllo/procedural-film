# Ortofon — record / stylus care and dust

Checked: 2026-09-27

Sources:
https://ortofon.com/pages/protect-your-equipment
https://ortofon.com/pages/faq

## Source-derived facts

- Ortofon recommends keeping records clean and free of dust.
- Ortofon states that dust and dirt can increase stylus wear and that record cleaning improves the listening experience.
- A record brush can remove dust/dirt from the record surface.
- Stylus cleaning is a separate maintenance action and should be performed gently.
- The stylus is delicate and should not be touched with fingers.
- Ortofon recommends periodic use of a record-cleaning machine as part of record/stylus care.

## Story implications

The “problem” shot can show:
- dust in the groove field;
- a visibly contaminated stylus/groove macro as an explanatory inset;
- the listener reacting to surface noise while the record plays.

The film should avoid claiming that all noise is caused only by dirt. Scratches, wear, setup and other causes exist, so V3 frames this as one dirty record that improves after cleaning.

## Graphic implications

Dirty playback:
- irregular short crackle marks;
- small dust particles near groove/stylus;
- unstable magenta/cyan registration offset used only as a perceptual comic effect.

Clean playback:
- stable contour;
- no random crackle marks;
- smoother rhythmic line/ring language.
